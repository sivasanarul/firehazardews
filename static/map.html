About the data

Follow the Area, Dates, and Layers steps, then select Explore map. After loading, select a fire marker for details or browse the Results tab. Use Adjust map settings to change your selection.

Fire detections · NASA FIRMS
Satellite thermal anomalies indicate possible fire activity. A detection is not a confirmed wildfire or a measure of burned area.
15-day event polygons
Buffered groups of nearby detections, not measured fire perimeters. Fire radiative power is shown in megawatts (MW).
Hazard index · 2025
SLIM continuous raster for preparedness, on a fixed scale from 0 (lower) to 1 (higher). This is a hazard index, not a probability or a risk assessment. Changing the observation dates does not change its year.
District boundaries
Spatial reference in all scenarios, using the supplied district coverage. Hover a district for its name and province. Boundaries are independent of the observation period.
Recovery status · 2025
Primary restoration layer. SLIM satellite raster marking areas detected as recovered over 2025. It is a binary classification and does not depend on an observation period.
Burned area
A satellite burn-scar mask published monthly (2025–2026) plus a full-year 2025 composite. Detection & response shows a chosen 2026 month of fire activity together with the burned-area mask of the previous month. Restoration always uses the fixed 2025 yearly composite. Cells are a binary burned/not-burned classification, not a severity or perimeter measure.
Environmental layers
GWIS supplies near-real-time burned area and vegetation fuel. SLIM land cover uses the year nearest your observation period. Precipitation uses the selected window ending on your “To” date.

Availability depends on the source and period. Missing data and failed requests are reported in the workspace; they do not imply an absence of fire.
