A GUIDE TO YOUR WORKSPACE

Understand the signals.

Choose an area, observation period, and data layers, then select Update map. Click a fire marker for its details, or browse the Results tab.

Fire detections · NASA FIRMS
Satellite thermal anomalies indicate possible fire activity. A detection is not a confirmed wildfire or a measure of burned area.
15-day event polygons
Buffered groups of nearby detections, not measured fire perimeters. Fire radiative power is shown in megawatts (MW).
Environmental layers
GWIS supplies burned area and vegetation fuel. SLIM land cover uses the year nearest your observation period. Precipitation uses the selected window ending on your “To” date.

Availability depends on the source and period. Missing data and failed requests are reported in the workspace; they do not imply an absence of fire.
